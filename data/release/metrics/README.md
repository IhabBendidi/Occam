# Per-split cell-eval metrics — every metric, every arm, every split

## What this is

The complete metric output behind Occam's reported numbers. Reports quote four metrics and their mean over 5 splits; this is all **35** metrics cell-eval 0.6.0 produced, for **35** arm-scorings, split by split, so any claim can be re-derived rather than trusted.

Benchmarks: norman_combo, rhj_fewshot. Generated by `scripts/export_split_metrics.py`.

## Scope

**Our published arms** (NQ02, Y06 — the two main checkpoints, `NQ02` under all 12 composition modes), plus **every baseline** and **every competitor arm**, on both benchmarks. That is `--scope published`, the default, and it is what is tracked in git.

The campaign's other ~360 eval roots (the rhj_fewshot loss/schedule sweep, the five non-published norman bases) are history rather than results. `--scope all` includes them and is what is published to HuggingFace as `metrics/`.

## Files

| file | contents |
|---|---|
| `metrics_long.csv` | tidy: one row per (benchmark, arm, compose_mode, scored_arm, split, panel, metric) |
| `metrics_wide__<benchmark>__<panel>.csv` | arms × metrics, split **mean** and **SEM** |
| `per_split__<benchmark>__<panel>.csv` | arms × metrics with **one column per split** |
| `per_perturbation/<benchmark>/<arm>/split_NN.csv` | cell-eval's own per-perturbation rows, for the published arms and every reference arm |

## Columns

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `benchmark` | `rhj_fewshot` (unseen perturbation) or `norman_combo` (unseen pair) |
| `arm` | the trained arm, e.g. `Y06`, `NQ02` |
| `compose_mode` | multi-perturbation composition mode; empty on single-perturbation benchmarks. **Part of the result's identity** — 12 modes share one checkpoint |
| `config_tag` | `rhj_fewshot` only: the run's full identity on disk, every varied axis included (loss terms, epochs, eta_min, seed) |
| `scored_arm` | what was scored: `model_nocal`, or a reference arm (`meanpert`, `transfer`, `sum_of_singles`, `experimental`, `ctrlmean`, `ctrlcell`) |
| `arm_kind` | `occam` (ours), `competitor` (a ported published method), `competitor_DEFECTIVE` (a known-wrong pass, kept as evidence — **never quote it**), or `reference` (a baseline) |
| `calibration` | `nocal` throughout. `cal` appears only with `--calibration all` and is **not** what is reported |
| `panel` | `all`, or a declared sub-panel (`additive`, `nonadditive` on `norman_combo`) |
| `n_perts` | perturbations in that panel, a cross-check on the row |

## Direction — which way is good

**Lower is better** (these are ERRORS): `mae`, `mse`, `de_nsig_counts_pred`, `de_nsig_counts_real`.

**Higher is better** for everything else. `discrimination_score_*` has chance at **0.5**, not 0.

⚠️ **Do not rank on these**, in either direction — they are context, not quality: `overlap_at_N`, `precision_at_N`, `de_nsig_counts_pred`, `de_nsig_counts_real`. On `rhj_fewshot` a zero-signal predictor beats a real model on `overlap_at_N` (the emission-floor artifact), and `de_nsig_counts_pred` vs `_real` differ by ~5x because a low-variance prediction inflates the Wilcoxon test's significance calls.

## Anchors — a score is unreadable without them

`rhj_fewshot`, `pearson_delta`: `ctrlcell` −0.0018 · `ctrlmean` 0.0016 · `meanpert` 0.1324 · `transfer` 0.2323 · `experimental` 0.3722. Published arm **Y06 = 0.4043**.

`norman_combo`, `pearson_delta` (additive / nonadditive): `meanpert` 0.4634 / 0.4016 · **`sum_of_singles` 0.8824 / 0.7757 ← the bar** · `experimental` 0.9165 / 0.8377. Published arm **NQ02 × decode_seq_mean = 0.8169 / 0.7380**.

⚠️ `experimental` is replicate agreement at the panel's own cell counts, **not an upper bound**: a model predicting the group mean can beat a noisy empirical half, and on `rhj_fewshot` Y06 does.

⚠️ A `pearson_delta` is a rank within its own panel. Do not compare a `norman_combo` number to an `rhj_fewshot` one.

| `txpert_mean` | 0.3343 | A/B diagnostic: `(cell_type, batch)` MEAN controls |
| `txpert_ep20` | 0.3038 | A/B diagnostic: epoch-20 checkpoint |

⚠️ None of these reproduces its method's published figure, and none is meant to: each carries deliberate, recorded deviations (`docs/rhj_competitors_results.md`, and the header of `scripts/repro/competitors_rhj.sh`). Read them as "how this architecture does on this task under this protocol", not as a reimplementation score.

## Which `norman_combo` panel is the reported one

**`additive` and `nonadditive` only.** Both are 36 pairs and are **effect-size-controlled**: the additivity residual `‖Δ_A + Δ_B − Δ_real‖ / ‖Δ_real‖` is terciled *within* effect-size terciles, so the two panels are matched on how large the true effect is. The `*_raw` panels tercile the residual globally and are therefore confounded with effect size — they are exported for completeness and **must not be quoted**. `middle` is the middle tercile, held out of the comparison by design. `all` is cell-eval's own aggregate over all 111 test pairs and mixes the strata in whatever ratio the panel happens to have.
