# Official Occam comparisons by frozen benchmark panel

Uncalibrated `model_nocal` scores. Every row has five training seeds (42–46) and five splits (0–4) per seed. For each metric, average the splits within each seed, then report the mean and sample SD (`ddof=1`) across the five seed means. Larger is better except MAE. Discrimination L1 chance is 0.5.

Rows are ranked **within each panel** by the sum of four metric ranks: Pearson Δ, overlap@N, and discrimination L1 descending; MAE ascending. A smaller rank sum is better. Tied metric values receive average ranks; remaining ties are resolved by Pearson Δ, then MAE. This ordering is a descriptive four-metric comparison, not a new benchmark score.

Official Occam arms: `ABL_N_ALL` (GO+STRING + GenePT + ESM2, smooth-L1 + 0.5 delta-cos) for RHJ/H1/K562; `NF32ALLA` (the same sources, NF32 schedule, smooth-L1 + 0.0255 delta-cos, `decode_seq_mean`) for Norman. Norman's `additive` and `nonadditive` panels are the headline strata. Its `_raw`, `middle`, and `all` panels are displayed separately and are not pooled into those strata.
