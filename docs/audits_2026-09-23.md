⚠️ **The zero-fill of the axis genes a panel does not measure is NOT neutral on H1.** The 448
RHJ-axis genes absent from H1 are highly expressed in training (mean 1.05 vs 0.40 for present
genes). A zero there is a fake strong down-regulation. For CellFlow (centred PCA) the offset it
induces is 27.2 in PCA norm against a median input norm of 41.8 (~65 %); on K562 (343 absent genes,
mean 0.20) it is 2.5 vs 15.2. CellFlow's decoded-vs-input control gene-mean correlation: H1 0.54,
K562 0.97.

**This applies to every arm, not only CellFlow**: the zero-fill lives in the shared
`load_panel_split` (competitors) and in Occam's `enc_cols` scatter (Occam puts the 448 at 0 like the
37k non-axis genes, but these 448 are axis genes it was trained to see expressed). It may explain
part of every model's H1 collapse, including Occam's 10× `mae`. The neutral alternative is to fill
absent genes with the training mean (zero contribution after centring). This is a protocol
decision, not applied — see STATUS / h1_seen.md.

### Occam — Part A: NO LEAK; Part B: wiring OK, ⛔ H1 input is outside the training input space

**Part A (leaks), read from real checkpoints:** NF31 (norman_combo, 8,000 genes, K562, 131 pairs
excluded; train 105 singles / val 20 / test 111, all disjoint), Y06 and RB512 (rhj_fewshot, 6,983
genes, HEPG2, 724 perts excluded). `exclusions.json` = val ∪ test on both; the stream drops those
(file, pert) cells; training refuses a split that differs from splits.json; selection is on the
val loader only; run dirs are `runs/<config>_<suffix>` so rhj/norman cannot collide; evals refuse a
checkpoint whose axis / gene count / benchmark id differ from the panel; daemons trigger on
`final_model.pth` and score `best_model.pth`. Not a leak but recorded: most norman arms before
NA04/NF30 (NA00–03, NA05–08, NB, NC, ND, NE, NM03, NM06, NQ02) were SELECTED on `sum` while scored
on `decode_seq_mean` (NA04 and NF30 showed the selected epoch did not change for NQ02 / NE25).
CANNOT TELL: whether the DiverseRNA pretraining corpus (7 of 9 blocks unattributed) contains
evaluation cell lines — the model card already states this limitation.
Latent risks: daemon ledgers key on arm id only (a re-trained arm under a new tag would not be
re-scored); `runs/norman_combo/eval/<ARM>__<COMPOSE>` has no tag (two tags of one arm would
overwrite). Neither has occurred.

**Part B (OOD panels):** model input = the panel's own half-B controls (19,088 H1 / 5,345 K562;
100 % `non-targeting`, the panels' own batches); axis genes placed at their encoder columns by
Ensembl id; scoring on exactly 6,535 / 6,640 genes; no cell-line input (`source_cond` none);
context_shift.json and metrics.json of all 20 H1 splits consistent (80 perts, 33,559 rows).

⛔ **Two input-space problems on H1** (K562 only mildly affected):
1. **Zero-fill of absent axis genes** (as above): the 448 genes H1 lacks carry **21.6 %** of HepG2's
   axis log1p mass and include the most expressed genes (MT-CO1, EEF1A1, RPL13, RPL11, RPS18, RPS12,
   RPS2, ~2.2–3.0 log units in HepG2). K562 lacks 343 genes carrying 1.9 % (ID3, HLA-A, …).
2. **Row-sum rule mismatch.** Training divides each cell by its total over its SOURCE FILE'S FULL gene
   panel (HepG2 9,624 genes; stream_v2.load_chunk_sparse). The panel builder divides by the total over
   H1 ∩ HepG2 (8,910 genes) — in HepG2 cells those genes hold only a median **72 %** of counts, because
   the 714 missing ones are the heavy hitters. So every H1 input is inflated ~1/0.72 ≈ **1.39×** before
   log1p (~+0.33 log units on expressed genes). K562 ∩ HepG2 covers 96.6 % of K562's own counts (~3.5 %
   inflation). The builder's docstring claim of "the same gene universe" holds in name only.
   **Correction to docs/h1_seen.md and the builder docstring.**

Until fixed, the H1 numbers of EVERY arm mix the cell-line / assay change with this input
artefact. Candidate fix (a protocol decision, not applied): fill absent axis genes with the
training mean and rescale the row sum by the missing-mass fraction estimated in HepG2.
